# Recording and transcription policy worksheet

Unadopted public template. The deploying organization must establish permitted purposes, applicable
permissions, participant notice, objection handling and retention before use. No legal basis or approval
is supplied here; consult the [LIA](legitimate-interest-assessment.md) and [DPIA](dpia.md).

## Notice and objections

Prepare a notice that accurately identifies the controller/contact, purpose, capture/transcription mode,
local and remote recipients, storage, retention and rights route. Give required notice before capture,
including new participants. Verify any required affirmative permission; do not assume silence is consent.
An organizer-only in-app reminder does not itself notify participants or prove permission.

Example structure for private completion: "This meeting will be transcribed using Métis for [purpose].
[Describe local/cloud processing and recipients]. Records are stored at [location] for [period].
Contact [privacy contact] for questions or objections. [Explain available non-recorded alternative]."
Do not reuse it until every placeholder and actual capture mode has been reviewed.

Define and test immediate stop, off-the-record handling and deletion requests. Do not promise pause,
redaction, automatic deletion or recovery unless the selected installation demonstrates that behavior.

## Use restrictions and retention

Preserve a prohibition on participant emotion/engagement scoring and unreviewed consequential decisions.
Assess any proposed employment or monitoring use separately before enabling it. Require human review
of generated summaries and attributed claims. Keep transcripts, screenshots and derived records out of
unauthorized shares, connectors and logs.

Set justified periods separately for imported source audio, transient capture, transcripts, recaps,
entity records, audit logs, sync copies and backups. Verify configured retention settings and actual
sweeps; do not substitute example defaults for an adopted policy. Document suspension and incident
handling when notice, confidentiality, deletion or recovery cannot be assured.
