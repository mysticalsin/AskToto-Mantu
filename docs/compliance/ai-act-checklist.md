# AI deployment assessment checklist

Uncompleted public worksheet. It does not assign a risk tier, determine a provider/deployer role,
interpret an effective date or certify compliance. Responsible legal reviewers must assess the current
rules and actual use; no organizational approval is recorded here.

- [ ] Describe intended purposes, affected people, distribution model and responsible roles.
- [ ] Assess prohibited practices and employment/monitoring implications before enabling a use.
- [ ] Preserve the restriction against participant emotion/engagement scoring; verify prompts, schemas
      and relevant tests rather than treating an architectural statement as enforcement evidence.
- [ ] Determine applicable classification and obligations for each use case; record the reasoning privately.
- [ ] Assess participant notice, AI-content disclosure and provenance requirements; verify visible and
      machine-readable output behavior where required.
- [ ] Brief users on ASR errors, hallucinations, attribution limits, prompt injection, provider egress and
      the need for human review before relying on generated claims.
- [ ] Verify incident reporting, suspension criteria, logging, security and retention requirements.
- [ ] Record unresolved gaps and reassess when purposes, recipients, models, capture or publication change.

Cross-reference the [DPIA](dpia.md), [recording worksheet](recording-policy.md) and
[data-flow inventory](data-flow-onepager.md). Legal conclusions and dates belong in the reviewed private
assessment, with current authoritative sources; this template does not prefill them.
